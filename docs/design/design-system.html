What the research changed

Round 1 had the right content and the wrong craft: boxes around everything, flat hierarchy, too much mono. These are the changes, each tied to something I measured or read. Type values read size/line height in px.

What the references do

Borders everywhere
Linear separates with 1px white at 8% alpha and five text greys. Its write-up says “Structure should be felt not seen.”
Four text steps, one line colour, no table borders. Rows have no separators.
Sidebar as loud as content
Linear dims its sidebar so the main content leads: “Don't compete for attention you haven't earned.” Attio also insets its main panel.
Sidebar sits on a darker canvas. The main panel is one step brighter and inset by 8 px.
Condensed caps column heads
Mercury's table heads are sentence case, 13/20, weight 400, muted, in a 40 px header row. Linear's labels are 12/14, weight 510.
Sentence case, 12/16, in the third text step. The active sort column is one step brighter.
Mono for every value
Linear sets issue IDs in its UI face, Inter, with tabular numerals at 12/14, weight 510.
Tabular numerals in the UI face for dates and sizes. Mono only for hashes, Message-IDs and raw source.
Filled status pills
Linear removed coloured icon backgrounds in its refresh, and its issue labels use a small coloured dot, not a coloured fill. In the Mercury table I read, red appears only on a Failed tag.
A 6 px dot plus a word. Colour appears only when it signals a state.
Flat font weights
Linear's main weights are 400, 510 and 590. Its 20 px heading is weight 590 with −0.24 px tracking.
The same three weights. Tracking tightens only at 14 px and above.
Every number equally loud
Mercury sets cents smaller than dollars, so the amount reads first.
Units are dimmer than values: 2.5 MB. “Seen” stays blank until a message has been seen twice.
Bootstrap-like buttons
Linear's sign-up button is 32 px high with 12 px padding, light neutral on dark. Attio's is 36 px with a 10 px radius. Neither page leads with a blue button.
Controls are 28 or 32 px. The primary button is the text colour, so blue stays free for focus and links.
Static mock-ups
Linear shows a “1 / 84” pager with arrows and predictable header actions. Attio's sidebar has a Quick Actions ⌘K row.
Search, list keys, the pager, the command menu, selection and delete all work above.
Hand-set themes
Linear generates themes from three variables (base, accent, contrast) instead of 98, in LCH.
Two hues in OKLCH feed both themes, and no colour is set by hand elsewhere.

Sources: Linear and Attio homepages, which render product-like UI, not logged-in apps. Mercury's public demo of its banking app. Linear's UI redesign, design refresh and changelog write-ups. Resend, Tailscale and Superhuman showed me mostly marketing pages, so they shaped little.

Where MailArkiv departs from them

Those products manage work or money. MailArkiv holds evidence and untrusted content, so five things go a different way.

All text stays at 4.5:1 or better

Linear's dimmest label measures 3.45:1 on its dark canvas. People read an archive to check facts, so the lowest text step here stays readable. The fourth grey is for icons and disabled controls only.

State never hides

Linear gets calm by removing things. Here that would mislead: a search result must say what it can't see. Capture time, index lag and failures stay in the sidebar, and every search line reports the lag.

Mail sits on its own light sheet

Email HTML assumes white and may be hostile. It stays on a light sheet in every theme, in a sandboxed iframe in the real product, and the “this is mail” label sits outside it.

No sender avatars

Mercury's initials circles work because a bank knows its counterparties. A From header is only a claim, and fetching a picture would be a remote call. Senders appear as written.

The hash is a real object

A 64-character string is unreadable as one run. It appears in four-character groups you can compare by eye, and the copy button gives the plain hash. Next to it, a claim we can make: “Matched at last check”.

Decisions

Made by the project owner on 6 November 2026. Exact values are in frontend/src/lib/styles/tokens.css, and the rules are in frontend/DESIGN.md. If this page and those files disagree, the files win.

  1. GraphiteCool greys, 13 px type and one-line 38 px rows. At 1440 by 900 the archive list shows 16 rows. Below 1040 px wide it switches to two-line rows.
  2. Light and dark in Slice 1The product follows the system setting and has no switch of its own yet.
  3. Inter, with Geist Mono for evidenceInter covers Greek and Cyrillic. Linear's cv01 and ss03 alternates aren't in the web-font build we bundle, so this is Inter's default drawing.
  4. Lucide icons through unplugin-iconsEach imported icon is inlined at build time, so nothing is fetched while the app runs.

What this page doesn't prove: I tested in Chromium only, with no screen reader. Linear and Attio values come from public pages, not their logged-in apps. The message preview here is a plain element, while the product needs the sandboxed iframe. This page loads its fonts from Google Fonts for preview, and the product bundles them.
